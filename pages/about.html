Our Story

Okim Dive was born from a love of the underwater world and a belief that diving can become part of a much bigger journey. Founded by Iris after falling in love with Tioman's reefs, Okim Dive brings together learning, practice, and exploration.

From your first course to your next adventure, we are here to help you build confidence, keep diving, and discover more of the ocean. Tioman is our base, but there is always more to explore.
