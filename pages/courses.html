Frequently Asked Questions

Starting something new can feel uncertain. Here are a few answers to help you arrive with more confidence.

Can I learn to dive if I’m not a strong swimmer?

Talk to us about your experience first. You should be comfortable in the water and able to swim and float. If you’re nervous, you can start by practising basic floating or swimming in a pool and discuss the best approach with your instructor.

Will the course be too difficult for me?

Feeling nervous at first is completely normal. Training is progressive, and your instructor will explain each skill before you practise it. Everyone learns at a different pace, so the focus is on building your confidence—not comparing yourself with others.

Can older participants learn to dive?

Age alone is not always a limitation, but your health and comfort in the water matter. Please share any health concerns with us. If you are unsure about your fitness for diving, seek medical advice before booking.

Why can instructors seem serious sometimes?

Your instructor’s first priority is your safety. They may be especially focused during underwater practice. If you are unsure about a step or feel nervous, please ask—clear communication makes learning safer and easier.

How can I protect myself from the sun?

Bring a long-sleeved rash guard or sun-protective clothing, stay hydrated, and use reef-conscious sunscreen when needed. Try to prevent sunburn and take regular breaks between activities.

What if my ears feel uncomfortable while descending?

Changes in pressure can make your ears feel uncomfortable, especially when you’re new to diving. Your instructor will explain equalisation and guide you from shallow water. Never force a descent—tell your instructor immediately if you feel pain or cannot equalise.

Which certification organisation should I choose?

There are several recognised organisations, including PADI, SSI, NAUI, and CMAS. Okim Dive currently offers PADI certification. If you’re unsure which course fits your goals, contact us and we’ll help you choose.

How many days does a diving course take?

It depends on the course, your progress, and whether you complete theory work in advance. Open Water training usually takes several days for theory, skills practice, and open-water dives. We recommend allowing some flexibility so you can learn comfortably.

Do I need to buy diving equipment?

Course packages generally provide the essential equipment, so beginners do not need to buy a full set. You may want to bring or later choose items such as dive socks, a rash guard, a mask, or fins after you understand your own needs.

Still have questions? Tell us about your experience and we’ll be happy to help.
