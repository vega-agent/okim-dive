Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a course?

Contact us via WhatsApp or Instagram DM with your preferred dates and course type. We'll confirm availability and send you booking details.

What's the best time to visit Tioman?

The diving season runs from March to October, with the best visibility typically from April to September. Contact us for current conditions.

Do you provide equipment?

Yes, all equipment is included in course fees. For certified divers, you can bring your own or rent from us.

Can I bring non-diving friends/family?

Absolutely! We can arrange snorkeling, island tours, and other activities for non-divers.
