Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a good swimmer?

You need to be comfortable in water and able to swim 200 meters (or 300 meters with mask and fins). You don't need to be an expert swimmer.

What's included in the course fee?

All courses include equipment rental, learning materials, pool/confined water sessions, open water dives, and certification fees.

How long is the certification valid?

PADI certifications are valid for life. However, we recommend refresher courses if you haven't dived in over a year.

Are lessons conducted in Mandarin?

Yes, lessons are conducted in Mandarin. Please let us know your preference when booking.
