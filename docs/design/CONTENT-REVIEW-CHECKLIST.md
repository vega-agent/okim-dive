# Okim Dive — Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist before approving any new or edited copy, UX text, page section, image caption, or image-generation prompt.

## Purpose and ownership

- [ ] What visitor question or decision does this content support?
- [ ] Is this the page that owns that question?
- [ ] Does the content already exist elsewhere?
- [ ] If the heading is already clear, have we avoided adding a repetitive supporting paragraph?
- [ ] Have we avoided a decorative kicker or starter line that adds no essential context?
- [ ] Can any existing content be removed or tightened instead of adding more?

## Okim Dive tone

- [ ] Does it feel warm, personal, and encouraging?
- [ ] Does it show love or respect for nature and the underwater world?
- [ ] Does it make safety feel calm and capable rather than frightening?
- [ ] Does it invite exploration without sounding extreme or competitive?
- [ ] Does it appreciate beauty without becoming vague or decorative?
- [ ] Is it concise, specific, and free from generic tourism language?
- [ ] Does supporting copy match the calm, personal voice of the surrounding page rather than sounding operational or package-led?
- [ ] Would this sound natural in Simplified Chinese after translation?

## Content truth

- [ ] Are all prices, dates, destinations, inclusions, exclusions, certifications, and operational details verified?
- [ ] Are sample or historical details clearly excluded from public copy?
- [ ] Are per-trip inclusions separated from additional costs, exclusions, or details still requiring confirmation?
- [ ] If equipment is not included, is rental clearly described as an additional cost rather than an inclusion?
- [ ] Have medical, safety, and certification statements been carefully qualified?
- [ ] Does the copy avoid guarantees such as risk-free, safest, perfect, or guaranteed certification?
- [ ] Does it avoid importing Okim Explorer's old general-outdoor, loyalty, merchandise, or payment content?
- [ ] Does it avoid sensitive information such as bank details or credentials?

## Visual and UX fit

- [ ] Does this content earn its space in the layout?
- [ ] Does it preserve one clear message per section?
- [ ] If the surface is translucent, is the watercolor/artwork visible while the copy remains immediately readable?
- [ ] Is the hierarchy obvious without extra labels or decoration?
- [ ] Is the copy readable at desktop and mobile widths?
- [ ] Are headings, body copy, buttons, forms, and captions using their intended roles?
- [ ] If an image is used, is it verified Okim Dive photography or clearly presented as illustration/atmosphere?

## Final decision

Choose one:

- [ ] Publish as written
- [ ] Publish after tightening
- [ ] Move to a different page
- [ ] Hold for business confirmation
- [ ] Remove because it adds no meaningful value
