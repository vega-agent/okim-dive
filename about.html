Meet Iris

Certified Dive Instructor

With years of experience exploring the vibrant waters of Tioman Island and beyond, Iris brings passion, expertise, and a deep love for marine life to every dive.

As a PADI-certified instructor, she has guided hundreds of divers—from complete beginners to advanced enthusiasts—through the magical underwater world. Her patient, personalized approach ensures every student feels confident and excited about their diving journey.

Our Story

Okim Dive was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves to experience the wonder of the underwater world. Founded by Iris, who fell in love with diving during her first encounter with Tioman's pristine reefs, Okim Dive has grown into a trusted name for diving instruction and adventure.

We're not just a dive center—we're a community of passionate divers who share a love for the ocean. Our mission is to make diving accessible, safe, and unforgettable for everyone, from first-time divers to seasoned adventurers.
