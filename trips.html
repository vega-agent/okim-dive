International Destinations

Premium

Sipadan Island

Malaysia

One of the world's top dive destinations. Famous for its dramatic underwater topography and incredible biodiversity.

  • Duration: 5-7 days
  • Depth: 5-40 meters
  • Group Size: 8-12 divers
  • Highlights: Barracuda tornado, turtle tomb, hammerheads
Inquire Now

Raja Ampat

Indonesia

The crown jewel of the Coral Triangle with the highest marine biodiversity on Earth. A diver's ultimate dream destination.

  • Duration: 7-10 days
  • Depth: 5-40 meters
  • Group Size: 10-15 divers
  • Highlights: Manta rays, wobbegong sharks, pygmy seahorses
Inquire Now

Similan Islands

Thailand

Thailand's premier diving destination with crystal clear waters and stunning granite boulder formations underwater.

  • Duration: 5-7 days
  • Depth: 5-35 meters
  • Group Size: 10-12 divers
  • Highlights: Whale sharks, leopard sharks, vibrant reefs
Inquire Now

Maldives

Indian Ocean

Pristine atolls and crystal clear waters teeming with marine life. The ultimate luxury diving experience.

  • Duration: 7-10 days
  • Depth: 5-40 meters
  • Group Size: 8-12 divers
  • Highlights: Manta rays, whale sharks, reef sharks
